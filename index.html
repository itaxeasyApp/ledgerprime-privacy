Last updated: 5 October 2026

What the app stores

LedgerPrime is offline-first. What you enter - your business details, ledgers, vouchers, customers and suppliers (names, phone numbers, GSTIN, PAN, addresses, bank details as you type them), items, GST figures and reports - is stored on your device. Documents you scan or attach (for example a PAN card, Aadhaar card, GST certificate, bank cheque, bank statement or invoice) are stored in the app's private storage on your device. We do not receive any of this.

How it is protected

Your data sits in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read, and is protected by your phone's own lock screen and storage encryption - so please keep a screen lock on. Your app settings are kept in Android Keystore-backed encrypted storage. The accounting database itself is not separately password-protected.

Permissions and what the app can access

The app asks for Internet and Network State only: for crash reports, for the PIN code lookup and to check connectivity. It does not ask for Camera, Contacts, SMS, Location, Microphone or Storage permissions. Photos are taken with your phone's own camera app or chosen with the Android Photo Picker; PDFs, CSV, JSON and Excel files are chosen with the Android file picker - the app receives only the file you pick. "Import from Contacts" opens the system contact picker, and the app receives only the one contact entry (name and phone number) you choose, to fill in a customer or supplier form. "Contact Us" opens your mail app; nothing is sent until you send it.

Scanning documents

When you scan a document, the text is read on your device by Google ML Kit text recognition. The picture and the text it finds are used only to suggest values for you to review and are not sent to us. Nothing is saved to your books until you review and confirm it.

Services we use

Firebase Crashlytics (Google) sends crash reports so we can fix bugs: the type of error and where in the code it happened, your phone model, Android version, app version and an installation identifier. The text of error messages is never sent, so your accounting data, GSTIN, PAN, bank details and amounts are not included. When you type a PIN code to fill in a city and state, the PIN code you typed is sent to the public service api.postalpincode.in, which returns the matching place. Google Play services supplies the on-device text recognition. The app shows no ads and uses no advertising or analytics SDK.

Sharing

We do not sell your data and do not share it for advertising. We share data only with the service providers named above for the purposes described, or when the law requires it. Files and reports you share, print or export leave the app only when you choose to send them.

Backups

Android may back up the accounting database to your Google account if you have Google backup turned on, or move it when you set up a new phone. Scanned documents, logos and app settings are not included in these backups.

Your choices and deleting your data

You can delete your business and everything in it inside the app (Settings > My Business > Danger Zone > Delete My Business), or remove all local data by uninstalling the app. We hold no copy of your accounting data on any server, so there is nothing for us to delete; there is no account to close.

Children

This app is a business accounting tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
