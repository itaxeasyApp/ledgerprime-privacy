LedgerPrime - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Who we are

What the app stores

LedgerPrime is offline-first. What you enter - your business details, ledgers, vouchers, customers and suppliers (names, phone numbers, GSTIN, PAN, addresses, bank details as you type them), items, GST figures and reports - is stored on your device. Documents you scan or attach (for example a PAN card, Aadhaar card, GST certificate, bank cheque, bank statement or invoice) are stored in the app's private storage on your device. We do not receive any of this.

How it is protected

Your data sits in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read, and is protected by your phone's own lock screen and storage encryption - so please keep a screen lock on. Your app settings are kept in Android Keystore-backed encrypted storage. The accounting database itself is not separately password-protected.

Permissions and what the app can access

The app asks for Internet and Network State only: for crash reports, for the PIN code lookup and to check connectivity. It does not ask for Camera, Contacts, SMS, Location, Microphone or Storage permissions. Photos are taken with your phone's own camera app or chosen with the Android Photo Picker; PDFs, CSV, JSON and Excel files are chosen with the Android file picker - the app receives only the file you pick. "Import from Contacts" opens the system contact picker, and the app receives only the one contact entry (name and phone number) you choose, to fill in a customer or supplier form. "Contact Us" opens your mail app; nothing is sent until you send it.

Scanning documents

When you scan a document, the text is read on your device by Google ML Kit text recognition. The recognition runs on your phone, and the picture and the text it finds are used only to suggest values for you to review; the recognition process does not send them to us (Itax Easy). Nothing is saved to your books until you review and confirm it.

Separately from your documents, Google's ML Kit may collect information about the app and your device - such as the device manufacturer, model and Android version, performance metrics, error codes and per-installation identifiers - which Google describes as used for diagnostics and usage analytics. This is information collected by Google's software, not by us, and according to Google's published list it does not include the pictures or the text of your documents. Google states that it does not transfer this data to third parties. See Google's ML Kit data disclosure.

Services we use

Firebase Crashlytics (Google) sends crash reports so we can fix bugs: the type of error and where in the code it happened, your phone model, Android version, app version and an installation identifier. The text of error messages is never sent, so your accounting data, GSTIN, PAN, bank details and amounts are not included. Firebase Sessions (Google), a second Firebase component, may also collect app information (such as the package name, Android and SDK versions and the type of network connection), your device manufacturer and model, and usage information such as how long the app was in the foreground to start a new session. Google describes this as used to provide crash and performance metrics for the app. See Google's Firebase data disclosure. When you type a PIN code to fill in a city and state, the PIN code you typed is sent to the public service api.postalpincode.in, which returns the matching place. Google Play services supplies the on-device text recognition; see "Scanning documents" for the diagnostic information that Google's ML Kit may collect. The app shows no ads and uses no advertising SDK. Crashlytics, Firebase Sessions and ML Kit do collect the diagnostic, performance and usage information described on this page, so the app does include Google software that gathers diagnostic and usage-analytics data.

Sharing

We do not sell your data and do not share it for advertising. We share data only with the service providers named above for the purposes described, or when the law requires it. Files and reports you share, print or export leave the app only when you choose to send them.

Backups

Android may back up the accounting database to your Google account if you have Google backup turned on, or move it when you set up a new phone. Scanned documents, logos and app settings are not included in these backups.

Your choices and deleting your data

You can delete your business and everything in it inside the app (Settings > My Business > Danger Zone > Delete My Business), or remove all local data by uninstalling the app. We hold no copy of your accounting data on any server, so there is nothing for us to delete; there is no account to close.

Children

This app is a business accounting tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
